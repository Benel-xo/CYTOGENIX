# Scientific Integrity and Limitations

CYTOGENIX is an experimental biomedical research and educational platform.
It is not a medical device and must not be used for clinical diagnosis,
screening, treatment decisions or patient management.

The platform must not fabricate model predictions, accuracy scores,
training progress, datasets, heatmaps, patient records or validation results.

Every model result must identify its model version, dataset provenance,
evaluation method and known limitations.

Demo and simulated data must be clearly labelled. Image measurements must
distinguish pixel units from physical units. Physical measurements require
valid image calibration metadata.

Dataset splitting must prevent patient-level leakage wherever patient
identifiers are available. Performance claims require documented,
reproducible evaluation.
